# Vendor brief — Meridian Object Store

Meridian is a commercial object store with an S3-compatible API. It
claims eleven nines of durability through erasure coding across three
availability zones, with a documented recovery objective of four hours.

Pricing is per terabyte-month with a separate charge for retrieval,
which makes it cheap for archival and expensive for anything read often.
